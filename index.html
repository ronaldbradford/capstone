Fairfield University Capstone Project

GenAI Under Attack: Evaluating Enterprise Security Gaps Against Emerging AI Threat Vectors

The integration of generative AI (GenAI) has introduced new attack vectors that existing cybersecurity frameworks were not designed to adequately address. This project draws on GenAI vulnerability reports from 2022–2025 and early 2026, cross-referenced against OWASP LLM Top 10 and MITRE ATLAS classifications, to evaluate which attack vectors current security controls leave most exposed and what mitigation strategies most effectively close that gap.
